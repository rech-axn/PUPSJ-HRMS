Terms & Conditions

Booking conditions, cancellation terms, and stay policies at PUPSJ Hotel & Restaurant.

1. Reservation & Guarantee Deposit

A guarantee deposit of 50% of the total accommodation charge is required to confirm room reservations. The remaining balance is payable upon check-in or upon departure at the front desk or through your Guest Portal.

2. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellations made up to 3 days prior to the scheduled arrival date receive a full refund of payments made. Cancellations made within 3 days of arrival will retain the first night's room rate as a late cancellation fee.

3. Check-In & Check-Out

Standard check-in begins at 2:00 PM. Registered guests must present valid government-issued photo identification. Standard check-out time is by 12:00 PM. Late check-out is subject to room availability and front desk confirmation.

4. Rates & Taxes

All rates posted on this website are in Philippine Pesos (₱) and include 12% Value Added Tax (VAT) and 10% Hotel Service Charge. Official BIR tax invoices are issued upon payment and folio settlement.

5. Guest Conduct & Hotel Property

Guests are expected to observe quiet hours between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM to preserve a tranquil environment for all residents. Any damages to room fixtures or hotel property will be charged to the guest's folio prior to departure.
