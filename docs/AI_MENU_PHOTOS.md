# AI menu photographs

Generated 18 October 2026 with the built-in image_gen tool. All 21 menu items have individual AI-generated images. The first image serves as a visual reference for the full series: pale warm oak, matte ivory ceramics, oatmeal linen, brushed brass cutlery, soft daylight from upper left, warm-neutral color and a 45-degree camera angle.

Full prompts: MENU_AI_PROMPTS.json. Active assets: public/assets/menu/ai/. Original generated PNGs are copied without image edits. The menu identifies each image as an AI-generated serving suggestion and does not present it as a photograph of an actual served dish. Old stock photo credits are retained in MENU_PHOTO_SOURCES.md for historical reference, but do not apply to the active AI images.

Menu names, prices, availability, and transaction records are preserved. External stock-source and license fields are cleared for the new images rather than assigning a stock photographer's credit to generated content.
