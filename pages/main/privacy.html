LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Information collection, handling, and guest rights at PUPSJ Hotel & Restaurant.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a reservation, reserve a dining table, or register an account, we collect necessary contact information such as your name, email address, contact telephone, and stay preferences. This information is used strictly to fulfill your booking, process check-in, and facilitate communication.

2. Payment Information

For your security, full credit card numbers and security codes are never permanently stored in plaintext within our digital platform. Payment records display only the card brand and last four digits alongside transaction authorization codes for verification.

3. Data Storage & Local Persistence

In this demonstration implementation, reservation records, dining orders, and session credentials are retained locally in browser storage for project simulation purposes. In a commercial environment, data is maintained within secured database systems compliant with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

4. Inquiries & Contact

If you have questions regarding your data or wish to request corrections to your profile, please contact our front desk at info@pupsjhotel.com or via our Contact page.
